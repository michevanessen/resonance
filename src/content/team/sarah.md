Sarah has over 10 years of experience in children's ministry leadership within churches and faith-based nonprofits. She specializes in developing engaging, age-appropriate curriculum, training volunteers, and creating safe, nurturing environments for spiritual growth. Sarah holds a Masters in Divinity and is a powerful speaker on the topic of child faith formation. She is also the co-creator of Parenting after Deconstruction.
