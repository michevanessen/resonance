sectionTitle: KNOW FIRST

approach:
  title: WHY DIAGNOSTIC FIRST
  paragraphs:
    - >-
      Organizations rarely fail at the level they think they are failing. A
      cash-flow problem turns out to be a donor concentration problem. A staff
      morale problem turns out to be a job description that has not matched the
      job for three years. A facilities problem turns out to be a budgeting
      problem that has been deferred for a decade.
    - >-
      So we do not prescribe before we understand. The first ninety days of any
      engagement are an assessment: finances, staffing, compliance, systems,
      facilities, and governance, examined together rather than one at a time.
      What comes out is a short, ordered list of what to fix and in what order.
    - >-
      The training behind that is in organizational leadership and the
      organizational life cycle — which failures are predictable at which stage
      of an organization's life. We use it as a diagnostic instrument. We do not
      sell it as coaching.
