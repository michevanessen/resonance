    - The day-to-day activities of most of the organizations we work with tend to tell a different story from the clear mission that they have. This gap is generally not due to a lack of vision; it results from factors such as budgets, payroll records, personnel files, filings, and various processes that have been left unrecorded and developed as different people have taken over responsibility for them.

process:
  title: HOW IT WORKS
  steps:
    - label: ASSESS
      question: What is actually happening?
      body: The first ninety days are diagnostic. Finances, staffing, compliance, systems, facilities, and the gap between the written policy and the real practice. We do not prescribe before we understand.
    - label: PRIORITIZE
      question: What has to be fixed first?
      body: A short, ordered plan that you approve. Sequenced by risk and dependency. What is most likely to hurt you, and what has to happen before anything else can.
    - label: EXECUTE
      question: Who is doing the work?
